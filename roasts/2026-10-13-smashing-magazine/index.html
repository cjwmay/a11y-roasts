Smashing Magazine 文章页：main 里套了个 main

Smashing Magazine Article Page: A Main Inside a Main

她是 Smashing 的真实付费用户（2026-08 一次买了 10 个 workshop），品类是教育/内容媒体。讽刺点拉满：一家以教人写出好界面闻名的出版物，自己的文章页也有结构问题 —— 无障碍树里 <main> 套着 <main>，读屏用户的 landmark 导航直接掉进俄罗斯套娃。方法：真机键盘走查 + DevTools Accessibility tree 取证。以下为 2026-10-13 抓到的版本。

She's a real paying Smashing customer (10 workshops in one go, Aug 2026); category: education/content media. The irony is thick: the publication famous for teaching good interfaces has structural issues on its own article pages — the accessibility tree shows a <main> nested inside a <main>, sending screen-reader landmark navigation into a matryoshka. Method: real-browser keyboard walkthrough + DevTools Accessibility tree forensics. This reflects the version captured on 2026-10-13.

①两层 <main> 套娃，landmark 导航变俄罗斯套娃Nested <main> landmarks: a matryoshka in landmark navigationMedium

现象

Accessibility tree 里，外层 main 直接包着内层 main —— HTML 大概是 <main>…<main>…</main>…</main>，内层那个还裹着文章正文、侧边栏和评论区。

用户影响

Landmark 导航是读屏用户的"目录跳页"（按 d 键跳）。嵌套的 main 让"跳到主要内容"的目的地变成两个，听到的结构是"main > main" —— 第一站可能根本不是文章。我在 DOM 里藏了个洞，用户却要买单。

WCAG 依据

1.3.1 Info and Relationships（best practice 级）—— ARIA 规范要求 main landmark 文档唯一、不嵌套。

修复方案

去掉内层 <main>：正文改 <article>、侧边栏改 <aside>，本来就是它们的语义。改的是 CMS 文章模板，一处修、全站几千篇文章全好。

What

The Accessibility tree shows a main directly containing another main — roughly <main>…<main>…</main>…</main>, with the inner one wrapping the article body, sidebar, and comments.

Impact

Landmark navigation is a screen-reader user's "jump to section" (press d). Nested mains make "jump to main content" ambiguous — users hear "main > main" and the first stop may not be the article at all. A hole hidden in the DOM, paid for by the user.

WCAG

1.3.1 Info and Relationships (best practice) — the ARIA spec requires the main landmark to be unique and unnested in a document.

Fix

Remove the inner <main>: use <article> for the body and <aside> for the sidebar — their actual semantics. One template change in the CMS fixes thousands of articles site-wide.

②h1 后面直接 h4，侧边栏小标题跳级h1 followed by h4: skipped heading levels in the sidebarMedium

现象

全页 h1 只有一个，是文章标题；但紧跟它的（侧边栏里）两个标题 "ABOUT THE AUTHOR" 和 "Email Newsletter" 都是 h4 —— h2、h3 被整个跳过。第一个真正的 h2 要到文章正文才出现。

用户影响

读屏用户按数字键扫标题层级时，"h1 → h4" 会被理解成"中间有两层被我错过了"，以为漏了内容；实际是侧边栏装饰性小标题级别给太高，噪音混进了导航。

WCAG 依据

1.3.1 Info and Relationships（technique G141）—— 标题应逐级使用，不跳级。

修复方案

把两个小标题改成 h2（侧边栏独立区块的语义就是 section 标题），视觉样式不变，导航立刻变干净。

What

The page has exactly one h1, the article title; but the two headings right after it (in the sidebar) — "ABOUT THE AUTHOR" and "Email Newsletter" — are both h4, skipping h2 and h3 entirely. The first real h2 appears only in the article body.

Impact

Screen-reader users scanning headings by level hear "h1 → h4" and assume they missed two levels of content; in reality it's sidebar eyebrow text promoted too high, noise injected into navigation.

WCAG

1.3.1 Info and Relationships (technique G141) — headings should be used without skipping levels.

Fix

Make both h2 (sidebar blocks are genuine sections). Same visual style, instantly cleaner navigation.

③每个小标题后都跟着一个叫 "#" 的链接Every heading is followed by a link named "#"Low

现象

每个 h2/h3 后面都带 <a class="anchor" href="#…">#</a>（复制章节链接用），无障碍树里暴露为 link "#"。读屏用户听到的就是："Real-World Design Principles，link，hash；下一个标题，link，hash……" 连续几十个。"#" 没有一丝信息量。

用户影响

本来方便人的"复制链接"功能，在读屏语境下变成贯穿全文的噪音；且它可 Tab 到达，回车还会跳到同页锚点 —— 一个"没名字、跳来跳去"的控件。

WCAG 依据

2.4.4 Link Purpose (In Context) + 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value —— 链接必须有可辨别的目的，纯 "#" 不满足。

修复方案

给它一个名字：aria-label="Copy link to section: Real-World Design Principles"。同样是模板一处修，全站生效。

What

Every h2/h3 is followed by <a class="anchor" href="#…">#</a> (for copying the section link), exposed in the accessibility tree as link "#". Screen-reader users hear: "Real-World Design Principles, link, hash; next heading, link, hash…" dozens of times. "#" carries zero information.

Impact

A helpful "copy link" feature becomes page-long noise in the screen-reader context; the link is also Tab-reachable and jumps to an in-page anchor — a nameless, jumpy control.

WCAG

2.4.4 Link Purpose (In Context) + 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value — links must have a discernible purpose; a bare "#" doesn't.

Fix

Give it a name: aria-label="Copy link to section: Real-World Design Principles". One template change, fixed site-wide.
